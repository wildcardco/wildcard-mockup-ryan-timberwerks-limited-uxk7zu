Custom Kitchen Cabinetry

Stock boxes come in three-inch steps, and your kitchen does not. We measure the room and build each cabinet to fit it. That covers the odd corner, the out-of-plumb wall, and the window that sits two inches off center.

You choose the wood, the door style, and the finish. We build the cabinets in our shop here in Mokena.

  • SizingMade to measure, non-stock
  • SpeciesYour choice of wood and finish
  • ForHomeowners & remodeling contractors

Bathroom Vanities

We build vanities sized to the room rather than to a catalog. You can add drawers where you need them and pick a height that works for the people using it. The wood is finished to stand up to a bathroom.

We work with single vanities, double vanities, and tight powder rooms. Bring us your measurements or have us take them.
